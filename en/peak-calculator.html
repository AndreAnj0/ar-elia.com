Idle capacity is the laundry's fixed cost, depreciation plus maintenance, on the part that goes unused over the year. "Covering the rest of the peak" is what each kilo you would stop sending out would cost, in equipment alone, if you raised capacity to August's level. The outside price is the same all year: in August the risk is not price, it is finding someone with capacity.

How we work it out.

The shape of the year comes from official data. Everything else is your choice. Here are the sums, so you can judge them.

The shape of the year
INE 2025
Overnight stays per day in Algarve hotels, month by month (indicator 0012088). August has 5.6 times January's stays per day, and June to September run 55% above the yearly average.
How it applies to you
Between two points
Each month sits between your weakest-month occupancy and your August occupancy, in the same proportion as the region sits between January and August. The same for linen per room. So the shape is the Algarve's and the amplitude is yours.
Why we don't use the curve as is
Hotels close
Part of the regional winter drop is hotels closing. A hotel open all year feels less than the region's 5.6 times, which is why the amplitude comes from your occupancy, not from the statistics.
Daily volume
rooms × occupancy × kg
Occupied rooms times linen per occupied room, in each month. The same rule as the ROI calculator, month by month instead of one average.
Idle capacity
Fixed cost × unused share
Each kg/day of capacity costs, per year, the investment divided by the depreciation period, plus 4% for maintenance. With the default values, €112. Idle capacity is that cost on the share of capacity that goes unused over the year.
Linen sent out
One price
In the months where volume exceeds capacity, the excess goes out at the price you chose, the same all year. We assume equipment cost grows in proportion to capacity, which is a simplification: in practice you buy in machine-sized steps.
Capacity used
Yearly average
Linen washed in-house over the year, divided by what the laundry could do working at its limit every day. Whatever is short of 100% is capacity paid for and idle.
